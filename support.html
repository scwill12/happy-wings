Frequently asked questions

How does measuring work?

On supported iPhones, Happy Wings uses the camera and Apple's ARKit to estimate your child's height from the floor to the top of their head. It's meant to be close enough and consistent over time — a charming ritual, not a clinical instrument. You can always fine-tune the number by hand, and on devices without the camera ruler you can simply enter the height yourself.

Is my data private?

Yes — completely. All measurements, photos, and keepsakes are stored on your device. Nothing is collected, and there are no analytics or tracking. Photos of your child never leave your phone unless you explicitly share or print them. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my keepsakes?

In Settings → iCloud backup, you can turn on backup to your own private iCloud account. This uses Apple's iCloud (managed inside your account) — we never see it. It's off by default. Changes take effect after restarting the app.

How do I share or print a keepsake?

Open a measurement, tap Create keepsake card (or use the Studio for collages and reels), then tap share. To print, choose Print in the Studio and send it to AirPrint or save the print-ready file — everything is made on your device.

The measurement seems off — what can I do?

Lighting, a clear floor, and holding the phone steady all help. Use the nudge slider on the review screen to adjust, or switch to entering the height by hand. Heights are most useful as a consistent record over time.

Does it work on iPad or older iPhones?

Happy Wings is designed for iPhone (iOS 17 and later). On devices without the AR camera ruler, measuring falls back to friendly manual entry — everything else works the same.

How do I delete a child or measurement?

Open the child's timeline or a measurement's detail and use Edit / Delete. Deleting also removes the associated photo from your device.
