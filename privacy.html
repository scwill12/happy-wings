Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Happy Wings does not collect your data. Everything — measurements, photos, notes, and keepsakes — stays on your device. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Our App Store privacy label is honestly "Data Not Collected."

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no information about you or your child. The app has no account and no login.

Where your information lives

All content you create in Happy Wings is stored locally on your device:

  • Children's names, birthdays, and optional details you enter
  • Height measurements and notes
  • Photos you take or attach (stored as files on your device)
  • Generated keepsake cards, collages, and reels

Photos of children

Photos never leave your device unless you take an explicit action to share or print one. There is no automatic upload, and the app never posts anything to social media on your behalf.

Optional iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup in Settings, your Happy Wings data is mirrored to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Apple manages this inside your account under Apple's privacy terms; we never have access to it. This setting is off by default.

Sharing and printing

When you choose to share a keepsake, it is handed to the app or service you pick (for example Messages, Mail, or AirPrint) through Apple's standard share sheet. When you choose to print to a print service, only the single image you are printing is sent, only at the moment you order, and we do not retain it. These are the only times your content leaves your device, and only at your request.

On-device intelligence

Features like detecting a face to frame a photo nicely, or estimating height with the camera, run entirely on your device using Apple's frameworks. No images or data are sent anywhere for this processing.

Notifications

If you opt in to gentle reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No information is sent to us or anyone else to deliver them.

Children's privacy

Happy Wings is intended for parents and guardians to record their own children's growth. Because no data is collected or transmitted to us, we do not knowingly gather personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
