Happy Wings

a keepsake of how they grow

Happy Wings turns the simple ritual of measuring your child against the wall into a beautiful, keepsake-quality record of how they grow — and lets you turn that record into cards, collages, and short reels to share with grandparents.

Privacy first  Everything lives on your device. Photos of your child never leave your phone unless you choose to share them, and nothing is collected or tracked.
